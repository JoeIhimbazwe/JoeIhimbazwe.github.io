Questions

Which music apps does it work with?

Spotify and YouTube Music work best. Aura also follows most apps that show up in Windows' media controls, like Apple Music, Boomplay and many browsers.

Do I need Spotify Premium?

No. Aura works with free Spotify too. Premium only matters for one extra: clicking a song inside Aura to start it straight away.

Where do the music videos come from?

From YouTube, played through YouTube's official player. Some record labels don't allow their videos to play outside YouTube. For those songs, Aura shows the album art instead.

Will it slow down my PC?

No. The whole app is about 2 MB, it pauses during games and full-screen apps, and it does nothing at all when no music is playing.

Can I turn features off?

Yes. Every feature has its own switch in the ··· menu: music video, edge light, lyrics, clock, song list and album cards. You can also start Aura with Windows, or not.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

Windows shows that for new apps from small developers. Click More info → Run anyway. It's a one-time step.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Aura asks for a licence key. Buy it once and everything keeps working. Your key works on up to 3 PCs.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet. Windows 10 and 11 for now.
